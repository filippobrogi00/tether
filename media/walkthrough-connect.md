## Connect in two steps

1. **Choose a server.** Hosts from your SSH config are listed automatically. Otherwise type `user@hostname`.
2. **Choose a folder on the server.** Click through the folders; no need to remember a path.

**Connect Folder…** pairs the two sides and starts continuous two-way sync. With an empty window, Tether creates a working copy on this computer and opens it. Because the files are local, every extension you have (language servers, linters, formatters, Git, AI assistants) works exactly as it does for any local project, and nothing is installed on the server.
