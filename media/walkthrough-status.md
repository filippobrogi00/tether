## One place to look

| Status bar | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Green `✓ myserver: in sync` | Every tracked file is up to date on both sides. |
| Yellow `⟳ myserver: syncing` | A create/edit/delete is in flight (or queued). Turns green when all finish. |
| Red `✖ myserver: …` | A conflict needs your decision, or a file/folder could not be created or synced. |
| `myserver: offline` | No connection. Keep working; changes are sent when it returns. |

Click the item for Sync Now, Remote Terminal, Toggle Remote View, Toggle Sync, port forwarding, and everything else.
